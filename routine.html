How to make a routine stick

Routines fail when they rely on motivation. They succeed when the next task is obvious, small and tied to something you already do. Attach your daily reset to an existing habit — after dinner, while the kettle boils, before you check your phone in the evening. Give each weekday a single focus so no session takes more than 20–30 minutes, and leave one day completely off so the plan survives busy weeks.

Work top to bottom and dry to wet: dust shelves before vacuuming, vacuum before mopping. Keep a small caddy with your core products and cloths in each bathroom so there is no setup time. If you share your home, put the printed plan on the fridge and split the weekdays — visible checklists reduce arguments about who did what.

Finally, be realistic. If you consistently skip a task, move it to monthly or outsource it. Many households book a professional deep clean every quarter and maintain the rest themselves — often the best value per hour of free time.
