Ways to pay less

Cleaning cost FAQ

How long does a house cleaning take?

A standard clean of a 2-bedroom home takes one cleaner about 2–3 hours. Deep cleans usually take 3–6+ hours and move-outs 4–8 hours, depending on condition.

Should I tip my house cleaner?

In the US, 10–20% is common for one-off jobs, or a holiday bonus of about one visit's cost for recurring cleaners. Check whether the company pays staff fairly — tips matter more when they don't.

Why is deep cleaning so much more expensive?

It covers what regular cleans skip: baseboards, vents, door frames, inside appliances, grout scrubbing and built-up grime — roughly double the labour.
