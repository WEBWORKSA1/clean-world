🪟

Ventilate smartly

Air out when outdoor AQI is good; keep windows closed during pollution spikes and wildfire smoke.

🌀

Filter indoors

A HEPA purifier sized for the room (check its CADR) and a MERV 11–13 HVAC filter cut indoor PM2.5 significantly.

🍳

Cut indoor sources

Use the range hood, avoid candles and incense on bad days, and vacuum with a sealed HEPA vacuum. Full guide.
