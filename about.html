🌍 About us

Clean is a habit. We make it easier.

Clean.World is an independent, ad-supported website with one mission: help people keep cleaner homes, breathe cleaner air and leave their communities cleaner than they found them.

What we do

  • Practical guides — original, step-by-step cleaning instructions with safety front and centre.
  • Free tools — a cost calculator, stain wizard, routine builder and DIY recipe library.
  • Live data — a Clean Air Index for 64 world cities and an auto-updated news feed.
  • Community — pledges, cleanup registration, safety kits and monthly contests.
  • Pro matching — free quotes from vetted local cleaning companies for people who'd rather hire help.

Editorial standards

Our guides are written and reviewed by the Clean.World editorial team. We favour methods that are effective, affordable and low-risk, we explain chemistry rather than repeating myths, and we flag safety issues clearly — especially chemical mixing, mould and ventilation. When a job needs a licensed professional, we say so. We review guides regularly and update them when products, standards or evidence change.

How we make money

Clean.World is free to use. We earn revenue from display advertising (Google AdSense), clearly labelled sponsorships, referral fees from cleaning companies that receive customer requests, and voluntary donations. Advertisers and partners never pay for favourable editorial coverage, and referral fees never change the price you pay.

Independence & name

Clean.World is the name of this website at its domain. We are not affiliated with any company, product, charity or campaign using "Clean World", "CleanWorld" or similar names. See our trademark & copyright disclosure.
