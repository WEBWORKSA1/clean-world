Official rules (summary)

  1. No purchase necessary. A purchase or donation does not improve your chances of winning.
  2. Eligibility: open to individuals 18+ where contests are legal; minors may enter the Kids' Art contest only through a parent or guardian. Void where prohibited. Employees of Clean.World and sponsors are not eligible.
  3. Entry period: each round runs from the 1st to the last day of the calendar month (23:59 UTC). One entry per person per contest per round.
  4. Judging: a panel scores entries on the stated criteria (40% impact/transformation, 30% helpfulness of tips/story, 30% presentation). Decisions are final.
  5. Prizes: as described; non-transferable; no cash alternative except at our discretion. Winners are responsible for any taxes. Prizes may vary by country due to delivery restrictions.
  6. Content licence: by entering you grant Clean.World a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to display your entry with credit. You keep ownership.
  7. Safety: entries showing unsafe practices (mixing chemicals, unsupervised minors at roadsides, handling sharps) are disqualified.
  8. Privacy: entrant data is handled under our Privacy Policy and used only to run the contest and, if you opt in, our newsletter.
  9. Winners are notified by email within 14 days of the round closing and must reply within 14 days or an alternate winner may be chosen.
