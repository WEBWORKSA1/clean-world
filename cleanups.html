The Clean.World pledge

  • 🌱 I'll pick up litter I pass — at least five pieces on every walk.
  • ♻️ I'll refuse single-use plastic where I can and refill instead.
  • 🧴 I'll choose safer cleaning products and never pour chemicals down storm drains.
  • 🌬️ I'll protect indoor air: ventilate, filter, and skip unnecessary fragrances.
  • 🤝 I'll join or host at least one community cleanup this year.

Cleanup safety kit

  • 🧤 Thick gloves for everyone + litter pickers
  • 🗑️ Separate bags: general waste and recyclables
  • 🦺 High-visibility vests near roads
  • 🩹 First-aid kit, water, sunscreen
  • ⚠️ Sharps rule: never touch needles or broken glass by hand — mark the spot and report to the local council
  • 👧 Adult supervision for under-18s; stay away from traffic, water edges and private land
  • 📸 Before/after photos + weigh your bags (bathroom scale works)
  • 🚛 Arrange pickup with your municipality before the day

Tip: share your before/after photos in this month's Cleanup Crew contest.
