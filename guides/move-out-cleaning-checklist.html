Landlords and property managers inspect the same places every time. Clean those properly, document everything, and you dramatically improve the odds of getting your full deposit back.

Before you start

Read your lease and move-in inspection report: some leases require professional carpet cleaning or specify standards. Clean after the furniture is out — you'll reach everything and won't recontaminate floors. Budget 4–8 hours for an average apartment and longer for a house. Work top to bottom and leave floors for last.

Supplies: all-purpose cleaner, degreaser, bathroom cleaner, glass cleaner, baking soda, microfibre cloths, scrub sponges, a magic-eraser-type melamine sponge, a vacuum, mop and bucket, a step stool, rubbish bags and a screwdriver (for vent covers).

Kitchen

  • ☐ Inside the oven, racks and door glass (see our oven guide)
  • ☐ Stovetop, burner grates and drip pans; under the hob if it lifts
  • ☐ Range hood and grease filter (soak in hot water and dish soap)
  • ☐ Fridge and freezer inside and out — defrost, wash shelves, wipe seals, pull out and clean behind/under
  • ☐ Dishwasher filter, door edges and seals
  • ☐ Microwave inside (steam a bowl of water and lemon for 3 minutes first)
  • ☐ Inside every cabinet and drawer, then the fronts and handles
  • ☐ Countertops, backsplash and sink, including the tap base and drain

Bathrooms

  • ☐ Descale shower glass, taps and showerhead
  • ☐ Scrub tub, tiles and grout; remove soap scum and mildew
  • ☐ Toilet inside, outside, the base, behind and the hinge area
  • ☐ Vanity inside and out, mirror, medicine cabinet
  • ☐ Exhaust fan cover (remove and wash)

Bedrooms and living areas

  • ☐ Dust ceiling fans, light fittings and corners for cobwebs
  • ☐ Wipe walls for scuffs and fingerprints (test the melamine sponge on a hidden area first — it can dull flat paint)
  • ☐ Fill small nail holes only if your lease allows it
  • ☐ Light switches, door handles, door frames and skirting boards
  • ☐ Windows, sills, tracks and blinds
  • ☐ Inside closets, shelves and rails
  • ☐ Vents and radiator covers

Floors and finishing

Vacuum everything, including edges with a crevice tool. Mop hard floors with a suitable cleaner and let them dry. For carpets, check whether a professional clean is required — if not, treat visible stains with our Stain Wizard and consider renting an extractor. Finally, take out all rubbish and recycling, replace any blown bulbs, and leave keys and remotes where agreed.

Document the result

Take time-stamped photos and a slow walkthrough video of every room, including inside appliances and cupboards. Keep receipts for any professional cleaning. Compare against your move-in report and note pre-existing damage. This evidence is your best tool if there is a dispute.

DIY or hire?

A professional move-out clean in 2026 typically costs about $250–$600 depending on size and condition. It often makes sense if you are short on time, moving long-distance, or your lease requires it — many companies will return free of charge if the landlord flags something.
