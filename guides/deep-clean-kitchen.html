The kitchen collects grease, crumbs and bacteria faster than any other room. A deep clean every few months — done in the right order — resets it in an afternoon.

The order that saves time
  1. Clear and soak
  2. Top surfaces and cabinets
  3. Appliances
  4. Counters and backsplash
  5. Sink and disposal
  6. Floor

1. Clear and soak (15 minutes)

Empty the counters, remove the range-hood filter and stove grates, and put them in a sink of very hot water with a generous squirt of dish soap (or a scoop of dishwasher powder for heavy grease). Start the fridge clean-out: bin expired food and put perishables in a cooler. Spread baking-soda paste in the oven now if it needs it — it will be ready by the time you finish.

2. Top-down: cabinets and high surfaces (45 minutes)

Dust the tops of cabinets and the fridge — a sticky layer of grease and dust lives there. Degrease cabinet fronts with warm water and a few drops of dish soap; for sticky build-up, a paste of baking soda and a little oil loosens it, then wipe with soapy water. Wipe handles and knobs, which are some of the most-touched surfaces in the home. On painted or wood cabinets, wring cloths well — excess water swells edges.

3. Appliances (60–75 minutes)

  • Fridge: remove shelves and drawers, wash them in warm soapy water (let glass reach room temperature first to avoid cracking), wipe the interior with a mild solution of baking soda and water, and clean door seals with an old toothbrush. Vacuum the condenser coils at the back or bottom to improve efficiency.
  • Microwave: heat a bowl of water with lemon slices for 3 minutes, leave the door shut for 5, then wipe.
  • Dishwasher: remove and rinse the filter, wipe door edges and seals, then run an empty hot cycle with a cup of white vinegar on the top rack.
  • Small appliances: descale the kettle and coffee maker; empty the toaster crumb tray.
  • Range hood: scrub the soaked filter, rinse and air-dry; wipe the hood exterior with degreaser.

4. Counters and backsplash (30 minutes)

Wash with warm soapy water, then rinse. For food-contact surfaces, follow up with a food-safe sanitiser or diluted disinfectant according to its label contact time, then rinse if required. Use stone-safe pH-neutral products on granite, marble and quartz — vinegar and lemon etch natural stone. Scrub grout lines on the backsplash with a baking-soda paste and a small brush.

5. Sink and disposal (15 minutes)

Scrub the sink with a baking-soda soft scrub, paying attention to the drain ring and tap base. Polish stainless steel with the grain using a drop of oil on a microfibre cloth. For a garbage disposal, run cold water and grind a handful of ice cubes with a little coarse salt to dislodge debris, then a few citrus peels for freshness — never put your hand inside.

6. Floor (20–30 minutes)

Pull out movable appliances if possible and vacuum underneath. Vacuum or sweep the whole floor, then mop with a suitable cleaner. Pay attention to the toe-kicks under cabinets, where splashes accumulate.

Food safety reminder: use separate cloths for raw-meat areas and other surfaces, wash cloths hot after every deep clean, and replace sponges regularly — they harbour bacteria.

How often?

Daily: wipe counters and the stovetop, wash dishes, empty the sink. Weekly: microwave, appliance fronts, floor. Monthly: fridge clean-out, hood filter, dishwasher filter. Every 3–6 months: the full deep clean above. Want a plan that reminds you? Try the Routine Builder.
