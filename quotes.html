Quote FAQ

Is this really free?

Yes. Requesting and comparing quotes is free and there is no obligation to hire. Cleaning companies may pay Clean.World a referral or advertising fee — this never changes your price.

How are cleaning pros vetted?

Partners must confirm business registration and liability insurance, agree to our conduct standards and keep a strong customer rating. We remove partners who receive repeated complaints. Always verify insurance and references yourself before you hire.

Should I choose hourly or flat-rate pricing?

Flat rates are easier to budget for recurring cleans; hourly rates can be cheaper for small or unpredictable jobs. Ask each pro what is included — inside the oven, fridge, cabinets and windows are often extras.

Do you handle commercial contracts?

Yes — offices, clinics, retail, restaurants, schools, warehouses and post-construction sites. Choose your property type in step 2 and describe your schedule; commercial requests are matched with janitorial firms.

Can I use eco-friendly products?

Absolutely. Mention it in step 4 and we prioritise green-certified providers. See our green cleaning guide to know what to ask for.
