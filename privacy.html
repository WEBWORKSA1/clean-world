Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy explains how Clean.World ("we", "us") handles information when you visit this website or use its tools and forms.

Information we collect

  • Information you submit: when you request quotes, apply as a partner, pledge, register a cleanup, enter a contest, donate, apply for a role, subscribe or contact us, we receive the details you enter (for example name, email, phone, location and your message).
  • Automatically collected data: like most websites, our host and advertising partners receive technical data such as IP address, browser type, device, pages viewed and referring URL.
  • Data stored on your device: some tools save preferences (theme, checklist ticks, routine settings) in your browser's local storage. This never leaves your device.
  • Location: the air-quality tools only use your location if you press "Check my location" and grant permission. Coordinates are sent directly from your browser to the Open-Meteo API to fetch air data; we do not store them.

How we use information

  • To respond to your requests and run the services you ask for (for example, sharing a quote request with up to three relevant local cleaning providers when you consent).
  • To run contests, cleanups, donations and newsletters you opt into.
  • To keep the site secure, measure usage and improve content.
  • To show advertising that keeps the site free.

We do not sell your personal information to data brokers. Form submissions are delivered to our inbox via a third-party form-processing service (FormSubmit).

Advertising and cookies (Google AdSense)

We use Google AdSense to display ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are shown a consent message (via a Google-certified consent management platform) before personalised ads or non-essential cookies are used. Choosing "Essential only" on our cookie notice requests non-personalised ads.

Third-party content

Embedded YouTube videos use the privacy-enhanced (youtube-nocookie.com) player and load only after you press play. Air data comes from Open-Meteo; news headlines link to third-party publishers. These services have their own privacy policies.

Retention and security

We keep submissions only as long as needed for the purpose you gave them, or as required by law, and use reasonable measures to protect them. No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA or PIPEDA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict use of your personal information and to object to processing or withdraw consent. To make a request, use our contact form and choose "Privacy request".

Children

Clean.World is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. Kids' contest entries must be submitted by a parent or guardian.

Changes

We may update this policy and will revise the date above when we do.
